Meet the Author: Drew Golden

Hello! My name is Drew Golden, and I am currently a freshman enrolled in Lehigh University's College of Business. Since my freshman year of high school, when I first got access to a brokerage account, I've immersed myself in the markets at every opportunity. I've fallen in love with the process of identifying and researching different companies as well as studying broader market data and trends. Through this blog, I hope to educate myself and others by diving into topics that I find engaging and relevant to current market conditions. Thank you for following along!
